#### 2. Reviewing Document Structure and Formatting

I reviewed the complete graduation internship report according to the Faculty's requirements. The review covered both the content and the presentation to ensure that the report was clear, consistent, and easy to follow.

The review checklist included:

* Chapter structure, section hierarchy, and connections between related sections.
* Titles, chapter numbering, section numbering, table of contents, and list of figures.
* Page margins, paper size, font family, font size, line spacing, and paragraph alignment.
* Page numbers, headers and footers, figure captions, and table formatting.
* Spelling, sentence structure, terminology, and consistency of writing style.
* References, URLs, and citation formatting throughout the report.

After the review, I exported the report to PDF and checked each page to identify line-break issues, layout shifts, missing images, or incorrect page numbering before printing the final copy.

#### 3. Finalizing and Submitting the Report to the Faculty

I completed the final version of the graduation internship report, obtained the required confirmations from the internship organization, and prepared the submission documents for the Faculty.

The submission process included:

* Sending the draft report to the mentor and supervisor for final feedback.

* Preparing the electronic file, printed copy, and related forms or documents.
