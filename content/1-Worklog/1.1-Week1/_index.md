### Internship Period

**May 04, 2026 - May 10, 2026**

### Week 1 Objectives

* Understand the company's organizational structure, AWS corporate culture, HR departments, and strategic partnerships.
* Establish a secure AWS account structure for learning and hands-on practice.
* Build foundational knowledge of core AWS services through official documentation and guided exploration.

### Internship Activities

#### 1. Organizational Orientation

I attended an orientation session covering the company's organizational structure, AWS corporate culture, and the roles and responsibilities of its HR departments. The session also introduced the strategic partners associated with AWS and provided an overview of their role in the broader technology ecosystem.

This orientation helped me understand the organization's working environment, internal coordination, and professional expectations during the internship.

#### 2. AWS Account and Identity Configuration

* Created an AWS **root account** as the initial account for managing the AWS environment.
* Reviewed the special privileges and security responsibilities associated with the root account.
* Configured a separate **AWS IAM account** for regular learning and practical activities.
* Applied granular permissions to the IAM account based on the required tasks and resources.
* Learned the importance of separating root account activities from day-to-day operations and following the principle of least privilege.

#### 3. Exploration of Core AWS Services

I explored and read the official documentation for several core AWS services to understand their purposes, primary features, and common use cases. The services reviewed included:

* **Amazon EC2:** virtual servers for running applications in the AWS Cloud.
* **AWS Lambda:** a serverless, event-driven compute service for running code without managing servers.
* **Amazon S3:** scalable object storage for storing and retrieving data.
* **Amazon RDS:** a managed relational database service that simplifies database administration.
* **AWS IAM:** an identity and access management service for controlling access to AWS resources.
* **AWS AI/ML services:** an overview of services that support the development and use of artificial intelligence and machine learning solutions.

### Week 1 Achievements

* Gained an overview of the company's structure, AWS corporate culture, HR departments, and strategic partners.
* Successfully created the AWS root account and configured a separate IAM account with granular permissions.
* Improved my understanding of account security, identity management, and least-privilege access control.
* Built foundational knowledge of the purposes and applications of core AWS services, including Amazon EC2, AWS Lambda, Amazon S3, Amazon RDS, and AWS IAM.
* Established the basic knowledge required for more advanced AWS labs and practical activities in the following weeks.
